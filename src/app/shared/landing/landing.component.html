Frequently Asked Questions

  • How does your Automated Trading AI work?

    Our Automated Trading AI uses advanced algorithms to analyze market data and make intelligent trading decisions on your behalf. It takes control of your trading account when you're absent, ensuring your investments are managed effectively.

  • How do I set up Stock Downward Alerts?

    Setting up Stock Downward Alerts is easy. Simply navigate to your account settings and enable the alert feature. You'll receive notifications when a stock you're interested in experiences a significant decrease in value.

  • How reliable are the Predictions & Analysis?

    Our Predictions & Analysis are based on rigorous mathematical models and thorough market research. While we strive for accuracy, please note that all investments carry a degree of risk, and it's important to exercise caution and do your own research.

  • What E-Learning Courses are available?

    Our E-Learning platform offers a range of courses designed to enhance your trading knowledge. Topics include technical analysis, risk management, and understanding market trends. You can access these courses through your account dashboard.
